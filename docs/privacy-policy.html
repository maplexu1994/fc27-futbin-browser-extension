1. 适用范围

本政策说明 FC27 FUTBIN Market Monitor 浏览器扩展如何处理数据。本扩展是 MapleShadow 独立开发的第三方工具，与 FUTBIN、EA 或 EA SPORTS 无隶属关系。本政策不替代 FUTBIN、腾讯问卷、GitHub、邮件服务商或浏览器商店的隐私政策。

2. 扩展读取和保存的数据

为监控用户选择的 FUTBIN FC27 PC 卡片，扩展会读取支持的球员详情页或 Market 页已显示的信息，包括球员名称、评分、卡片版本、卡片及基础球员 ID、页面 URL、PC 可见报价、Latest Sales 成交字段与时间、EA 价格范围、来源更新时间和采样时间。页面内容解析在本机运行；不将整个页面发送给开发者。

扩展检查当前所选标签页的 URL，并按 FUTBIN 地址查询对应监控页，用于添加卡片、切换或固定 Market 标签、刷新后台页和保留同一卡片的单个监控页。扩展不建立全局浏览历史，也不上传浏览 URL。

扩展在浏览器的 chrome.storage.local 中保存被监控的卡片信息、提醒规则、启用状态、读取成功或失败的状态及错误信息、最新市场信息、提醒状态和历史样本。数据用于展示面板、判断通知条件、历史比较以及用户主动导出和恢复。

3. 上传、共享和第三方服务

当前版本没有作者运行的监控上传服务，不自动向作者发送监控数据，不使用分析或广告 SDK，不出售监控数据，不将这些数据用于广告、信用评估或与市场监控无关的用途。扩展不读取账号密码、支付信息或私人通信来执行监控。

打开或刷新 FUTBIN 页面会由浏览器正常向该网站及其页面使用的服务发起请求，网站可能接收 IP 地址、浏览器信息、请求地址及已有会话或 Cookie；这些请求按该网站自身规则处理。扩展不专门读取登录凭据或 Cookie，也不绕过网站验证。

“问题反馈”链接由用户点击后在浏览器打开腾讯问卷。问卷网站依其自身政策处理访问及用户主动提交的信息。通过邮箱向作者发送反馈，或通过其他平台联系作者时，作者会收到用户主动发送的内容并用于处理反馈。请勿提交账号密码等敏感信息。扩展不会自动把监控备份附加到问卷或邮件中。

4. 本机文件与通知

CSV 导出和 JSON 备份仅在用户主动操作时生成本机下载文件。恢复备份只读取用户选择的 JSON 文件，不扫描其他文件。下载文件由用户保管；如果用户主动将文件发送给他人，接收方可看到其内容。

桌面通知可能显示卡片名称、报价、成交或价格范围，并可能在系统通知中心保留或被旁人看到。用户可修改或关闭价格规则、关闭成交底价提醒、移除卡片，或在浏览器和系统设置中管理通知。

5. 保留和删除

每张卡最多保存 672 条历史样本，超出后裁剪旧记录。通常约每十五分钟记录一次，手动记录可能缩短覆盖时长；这不是自动按七天删除的保证。卡片配置、最新信息和状态在本机保留，直到用户移除卡片或清理扩展数据。

点击卡片“移除”会删除该卡的本机配置、状态和历史。用户也可卸载扩展并清理其浏览器扩展数据。此前导出的 CSV / JSON 不会因移除卡片或卸载而删除，需用户自行删除；从旧备份恢复会重新导入其中的数据。

6. 安全与政策变更

监控存储受浏览器扩展机制和用户设备访问控制保护，但不是扩展额外加密的保险库。请保护设备及导出文件。若未来版本增加数据上传、分析或其他处理方式，我们将更新本政策及商店声明，并在适用时提供必要的说明与同意流程。

1. Scope

This policy describes data handling by the FC27 FUTBIN Market Monitor browser extension, independently developed by MapleShadow. It is not affiliated with FUTBIN, EA or EA SPORTS. This policy does not replace the policies of FUTBIN, Tencent questionnaires, GitHub, email providers or browser stores.

2. Data accessed and stored

To monitor selected FUTBIN FC27 PC cards, the extension reads visible information on supported player and Market pages: player name, rating, card version, card and base-player IDs, page URL, visible PC quotes, Latest Sales fields and times, EA price ranges, source update labels and sampling times. Parsing runs locally; entire pages are not sent to the developer.

The extension checks the selected tab URL and queries matching FUTBIN tabs to add cards, switch or pin Market tabs, refresh background pages and retain a single monitoring page per card. It does not build a global browsing history or upload browsing URLs.

Selected cards, alert settings, enabled status, success/error status and messages, latest market information, notification state and bounded history are saved in chrome.storage.local. They support the panel, alerts, history comparisons and user-initiated export and restore.

3. Transmission, sharing and third parties

This version has no developer-operated monitoring upload service. It does not automatically send monitoring data to the developer, include analytics or advertising SDKs, sell monitoring data or use it for advertising, credit assessment or purposes unrelated to monitoring. Account passwords, payment information and private communications are not read to perform monitoring.

Opening or refreshing FUTBIN pages causes ordinary browser requests to the website and services used by those pages. Those services may receive the IP address, browser information, requested URL and existing session/cookie information under their own policies. The extension does not specifically read login credentials or cookies and does not bypass website verification.

The feedback link opens a Tencent questionnaire only when clicked. That website processes visits and voluntary submissions under its own policies. When users voluntarily email or contact the developer through another platform, the developer receives the submitted content to address the feedback. Do not submit passwords or other sensitive information. Monitoring backups are not automatically attached to feedback or email.

4. Files and notifications

CSV exports and JSON backups are created as local download files on request. Restore reads only the JSON file selected by the user and does not scan other files. Users control exported files; anyone they share a file with can access its contents.

Desktop notifications may contain card names, prices, sales or price ranges and may persist in the operating system notification center or be visible to others. Users can modify or disable price rules, disable sale-comparison alerts, remove cards or manage notifications in browser and operating system settings.

5. Retention and deletion

History is capped at 672 snapshots per card, trimming older entries when the cap is exceeded. Recording is normally about every fifteen minutes; manual recording can reduce the covered period. This is not a guaranteed seven-day deletion schedule. Card settings, latest information and status remain locally until the card is removed or extension data is cleared.

“移除” (Remove) deletes that card's local settings, status and history. Users may also uninstall the extension and clear its browser extension data. Existing CSV/JSON exports must be deleted separately and are not removed by uninstalling or removing a card. Restoring an older backup imports its data again.

6. Security and changes

Local storage relies on browser extension isolation and device access controls; it is not an additional encrypted vault provided by the extension. Protect the device and exported files. If a future version adds uploads, analytics or other processing, the policy and store declarations will be updated, with appropriate disclosure and consent where applicable.
